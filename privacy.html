Privacy

What Germinal stores.

Short version: there are no accounts, no names, no email sign-up, no cookies set by Germinal and no advertising or analytics trackers. When you share a canvas, we store the picture and how it connects to other canvases. That's about it.

When you share a canvas

Using the canvas and pressing Evolve happens entirely on your device. Nothing is sent to us until you press Share. At that point, your browser sends us:

WhatWhat it is
The canvas imageThe colour of every pixel, and whether each pixel is held by a selection.
The themeThe theme word or words for the canvas, such as “love”. Only lowercase letters, numbers, spaces and hyphens are kept.
The generation numberHow many generations the canvas has been through.
LineageIf you opened someone's share link first, the ID of the canvas you started from, so we can tell how canvases branch from each other.
A random device IDA random code such as dev_4k9x2m7q1b, created by your browser the first time you share and kept in its local storage. It isn't linked to your name, email, phone number or any account. It just lets us tell that several shares came from the same browser. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes it from your device.

We also record when the canvas was saved, give it a short share ID (the code in the share link) and count how many times its share link has been opened. The image shows which pixels are held, but not who selected them. Your affinity score and your share message aren't sent to us.

The latest shared canvas for each theme is shown on the live display, and anyone with a share link can open that canvas. Treat a shared canvas as public. The device ID isn't shown to other visitors.

Stored in your browser

Germinal uses your browser's local storage (not cookies) to remember a few things on your device:

  • the random device ID above (only once you've shared);
  • whether you've already seen the intro, the opening genesis and the instructions, so they aren't shown every time;
  • on the live display, a copy of the last canvas it received, so the screen still has something to show if the connection drops.

Apart from the device ID when you share, none of this is sent to us.

Other services involved

Like any website, Germinal relies on a few other companies. They receive your IP address and normal browser details when your device connects to them.

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and runs the small server code that saves and loads canvases. On the live display it also serves the code that draws the QR code (from cdnjs.cloudflare.com).
  • Supabase is the database where shared canvases are stored. Your browser never connects to it directly; our server code on Cloudflare does.
  • Google Fonts provides the typefaces, so your browser downloads them from Google.

Our own code doesn't store IP addresses. Cloudflare and Supabase handle requests under their own privacy policies.

If you use the share buttons for WhatsApp, Messages or email, they open that app on your device with a message already written. Nothing is sent until you send it yourself, and we don't see who you send it to. “Save canvas image” downloads a picture to your device and sends nothing to us.

How long we keep it, and removal

Shared canvases are kept so their links and lineage keep working. There's currently no automatic deletion. If you'd like a canvas you shared taken down, email hello@germinal.studio with its share link. On request, we hide that canvas from public view and replace its image with a blank placeholder. Its place in the lineage is kept, and canvases that grew from it keep their own pixels.

Changes

If what we collect changes, for example if analytics are added, we'll update this page first.
