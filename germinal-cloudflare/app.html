◆ Let there be light

In the beginning, the canvas is germinal — the mind's own darkness, structured noise with no meaning. But life cannot emerge from darkness alone. Every living system requires an infusion of energy. The sun is not metaphor: it is the condition of possibility. Without it, no chemistry, no replication, no selection, no love, no art. The first act of genesis is light. This is how meaning begins: not designed, but recognised. The same process that will guide your hand when you make your own selection. The meaning begins with you.

When you chose a partner, you selected for resonance — their way of seeing, their sensibility, the particular quality of their attention. That selection was not random. It was affinity recognition. How decay works

Each generation where your selection is not re-stabilised by a new contributor, your score for that region decays by 15%. This means historical contributions that no longer resonate gradually lose value, while ones that keep being independently re-selected compound in worth. Like immune memory — what the body keeps fighting for is what matters.

The auction

Each canvas state — a unique pixel arrangement at a specific moment — goes to timed auction. The highest bidder at close wins an exclusive license to produce one physical print. No reserve is hidden; the starting bid covers print and shipping costs. An anti-snipe rule extends the timer by 10 minutes if any bid arrives in the final 10 minutes — ensuring fair competition.

What you win

Not the digital file — a license to produce one print, in your chosen format, of that exact canvas state. The license includes a cryptographic certificate verifying you are the sole authorised print-holder for that state. The license is transferable: you can sell it, gift it, or pass it on. You cannot make additional prints or use it commercially.

Blockchain provenance

On payment, a token is minted on Base (Ethereum L2) recording the canvas state hash, the timestamp, the winner's wallet address, and the license terms. This token is the license. Transfer the token, transfer the rights. The record is permanent, public, and requires no trust in any single institution. Pay by card or crypto — blockchain verification is included either way.

Creator pool

20% of every winning bid goes to the creator pool, distributed to contributors whose selections are present in that canvas state, weighted by affinity score. High-precision selections that survived many independent generations earn the most. The precise selector who chose 12 pixels that persisted through 50 generations earns more than the broad selector who covered 10% and faded.

Future dates

You can bid on a canvas that does not yet exist. The winning bidder for a future date receives the canvas as it stands on that specific day — shaped by every contributor between now and then. It is the only artwork where the content is genuinely unknown at time of purchase. Uncertainty is the feature.
